The game I made for for the engine is a bullet hell survivor game. 
Similar to Vampire survivor the famous game I based it on.
The player spawns in the middle and enemies spawn from the edge of the screen and they walk towards you.
The controls are WASD to move.
W is up, A is left, S, is down and D is right.
The Player character will auto shoot bullets and every five bullets there will be a big bullet.
The bullets kill in one shot.
The enemies also shoot back as well as do touch damage.
the enemy bullets and their body do 10 damage and the player has 100 health points.
After getting hit by ten bullets the game will end.
There is a counter on the top left that keeps track of how many enemies you killed going up to 999.
